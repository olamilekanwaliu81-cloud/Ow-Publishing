The story

From book design to a full hybrid house

OW started where most good things in publishing start, with the craft. Over 3+ years, the work grew from formatting and cover design into something bigger: authors kept coming back not just for a beautiful book, but for help getting it published and sold. So the service grew to meet them.

Today OW Publishing House is a hybrid house with three clear lanes, self-publishing production and growth, help reaching literary agents, and local print-and-ship, built on a proven, hands-on workflow across Amazon KDP and IngramSpark. The same top-rated communication standard used with clients on every project runs through all of it.

What hasn't changed is the principle underneath: an author should end up owning everything, understanding everything, and never feeling like a number.

A clear company model

Professional publishing, with you still in charge

Use one service or connect several, production, publishing, growth, agent prep or local print. The specialists stay specialised; your book keeps one joined-up route from manuscript to market.

What OW is

A hybrid publishing house you hire to produce, publish and grow your book, across Amazon KDP, IngramSpark and the platforms that fit it. One accountable team, foreign and local, from first draft to real sales.

What OW is not

We are not a traditional acquiring publisher, and not a vanity press. We never take your rights, your royalties, your publishing accounts, or your copies. You stay in control the whole way.

The principle that runs through everything

We don't warehouse books

“We hold no stock, and that's not a limitation. It's proof we're lean, honest, and on your side.”

01

Lean is better for authors

No warehouse means no inventory to recoup, no pressure to over-print, and no markup hiding in your costs. Every naira you spend goes toward your book, not our storage.

02

We're not a vanity press

A vanity press pretends to "select" you, then bills you while keeping your rights. We're the opposite: a transparent service you hire, with your eyes open, and nothing dressed up.

03

You keep your rights and your copies

100% of your rights stay with you. Every printed copy is yours, delivered to you. We produce, publish and grow, the book never stops being entirely your own.

04

Honesty is the whole model

We tell authors the truth, about costs, about timelines, about what agents can and can't promise. Trust you can defend beats a pitch you have to oversell.
