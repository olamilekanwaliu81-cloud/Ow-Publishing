Standing out as an author in the AI era

A plain-English guide · 5 min read

AI-generated manuscripts are flooding submission pipelines and store shelves. That's not a reason to panic, it's a reason to understand exactly where your advantage is, and lean into it hard.

The flood is real

The barrier to producing a book has collapsed. Anyone can now generate a passable-looking manuscript in an afternoon, and it shows: retailers and agents are drowning in samey, soulless titles. A new wave of scams has arrived alongside it, "AI publishing packages" that promise the moon and deliver nothing.

When everyone can make more of the average, the average becomes worthless. Scarcity moves somewhere else.

Use AI for the mechanical, not the meaningful

AI is a genuinely useful assistant for the boring parts. Let it help with:

  • A first pass at grammar and consistency.
  • Formatting chores and reformatting between versions.
  • Brainstorming angles, titles, or outlines you then judge yourself.

What you should never outsource is the thing readers actually come for.

Double down on the one thing AI can't fake: your actual voice, your specific stories, the way only you see the thing.

Your voice is the moat

A model can imitate a style in general; it can't be you in particular, your exact experiences, your point of view, the small true details that make a reader trust you. In a flooded market, that specificity is the whole game. The more generic content there is, the more valuable a real, human, unmistakable voice becomes.

Getting read without gimmicks

Visibility in this era isn't about chasing virality. It's grounded and slow: build a small, real audience before launch, use reviews and word of mouth honestly, and let genuine readers carry the book. A hundred people who truly connect with your voice will do more than a thousand who scrolled past an AI-written blur.
