Why your book cover matters more than you think

Your cover is the first thing a reader judges — often in a thumbnail the size of a postage stamp. It’s the single marketing asset working for you on every store, ad and shelf. Readers really do judge a book by its cover, and a weak one quietly costs you sales.

A professional cover does three jobs at once: it signals your genre instantly, it stands out in a crowded thumbnail, and it reads clearly at any size. Getting that right takes a designer who understands books and markets, not just graphics.

We design covers true to your story and your readers, built as a full wrap for print (front, spine and back) to the exact size your page count requires — so it fits perfectly and never gets rejected.

Good to know

Book Cover Design FAQs

Can I just design my own cover?

You can, but a DIY cover is usually the fastest way to look self-published. A professional cover signals your genre, stands out in a thumbnail and reads at any size — it’s the asset that earns the click.

What’s a full-wrap cover?

A full-wrap is the complete print cover — back cover, spine and front — designed as one file sized to your exact trim and page count, with the barcode placed correctly. Ebooks only need the front.

How many revisions do I get?

We work from your brief through revisions until the cover is right. You approve the final design before anything is published.

Do you design covers for any genre?

Yes — fiction, non-fiction, memoir, faith, children’s and more. We match the look to your genre and the readers you want to reach.

Do I keep my rights?

Always, 100%. Your cover is yours. Designing it with OW never transfers ownership of your book or its artwork rights to us.
