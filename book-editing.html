What is book editing — and do you really need it?

Book editing is the professional polish between “I finished writing” and “this is ready for readers.” An editor reads your manuscript the way your audience will and removes whatever trips them up — a plot that wanders, sentences that feel clunky, a character’s name spelled two ways, or a typo on page 212.

If you are a first-time author, here is the honest answer: yes, every book that sells well has been edited. It is the single biggest difference between a book that looks self-published and one that looks professional. Good editing is invisible — readers simply find your book easy to trust and hard to put down.

At OW we match the right level of editing to your manuscript and your budget, keep your voice completely intact, and give you tracked changes you review and approve. Nothing is changed behind your back.

Explained simply

The four types of book editing we do

Editing happens in order, from the biggest changes to the smallest. You may not need all four — we’ll tell you honestly which ones your book does.

1

Developmental editing

What it fixes: the big picture — structure, plot or argument, pacing, gaps, and anything a reader will struggle with.

Choose it when: your draft is complete but you’re not sure it all works as a whole.

Deepest edit
2

Line editing

What it fixes: how the book reads sentence by sentence — flow, clarity, rhythm, tone and word choice.

Choose it when: the structure is right, but the writing feels flat, wordy or uneven.

Sentence level
3

Copyediting

What it fixes: grammar, spelling, punctuation, consistency of names and facts, and one house style throughout.

Choose it when: the book is finished and you want it correct and consistent.

Correct & consistent
4

Proofreading

What it fixes: the final check of the laid-out pages — typos, missed words and layout slips before printing.

Choose it when: always — it’s the last step before your book goes to print.

Final polish

Good to know

Book editing FAQs

Do I need editing if I’ve already self-edited?

Almost always, yes. You are too close to your own words to catch everything — a fresh, trained pair of eyes finds what you can’t. At minimum, every book should be professionally proofread before it’s printed.

Will editing change my voice?

No. Our job is to make you clearer, not to rewrite you. We edit with tracked changes and an edit letter, and you approve every change. Your voice stays yours.

How long does editing take?

It depends on length and the level of edit. A copyedit of a typical book is faster than a full developmental edit. We give you a clear timeline with your quote, and editing usually runs inside a full 4–6 week production schedule.

Which level of editing should I choose?

Use the quick guide above, or just send us your manuscript — we’ll read a sample and recommend the right level honestly, including whether it’s cheaper as part of a package.

Do you edit faith and children’s books?

Yes. We edit devotionals, Bible studies and ministry books (and can check your Scripture references), as well as children’s books, memoir, business and fiction — matched to a specialist editor in your genre.

Do I keep my rights?

Always — 100%. Paying OW for editing never transfers ownership. Your manuscript, your rights and your royalties stay entirely yours.
