Good editing does not rewrite you — it makes you clearer. OW coordinates the right level of editing for your book and your budget, from big-picture structure down to the last comma, always keeping your voice intact.

Whether you are self-publishing with us or preparing to query agents, we get your manuscript to a professional standard first, because every other step — cover, formatting, marketing — works better on clean copy.

What book editing with OW covers

  • Developmental editing — structure, pacing, plot or argument, and what to cut or strengthen.
  • Line editing — clarity, flow and tone at the sentence level.
  • Copyediting — grammar, consistency, style and fact-checks against a style sheet.
  • A clear edit letter and tracked changes you review and approve.
