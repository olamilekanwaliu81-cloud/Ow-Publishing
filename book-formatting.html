What is book formatting — and why does it matter?

Formatting is preparing your finished manuscript into the exact file formats that printers and online stores accept: a print-ready PDF for paperback or hardcover, and a reflowable ebook (EPUB) for Kindle, Apple Books and Kobo.

This is the step where many first-time self-publishers get stuck — files rejected at upload, text falling into the gutter, fonts that won’t embed, an ebook that looks broken on a phone. It’s fiddly, technical, and unforgiving.

We handle all of it. You send your manuscript; we return clean print and ebook files that match your cover, pass platform review, and look professional everywhere a reader opens them.

Good to know

Book Formatting FAQs

Why do my files keep getting rejected at upload?

Usually trim/bleed mismatches, un-embedded fonts, or margins that are too tight. Professional formatting builds to each platform’s exact spec so your files are accepted the first time.

What’s the difference between a PDF and an EPUB?

A print PDF is fixed — every page looks identical, ideal for paperback. An EPUB is reflowable — it adapts to any screen and font size, which is what ebooks need. You usually want both.

Can you format a book with lots of images?

Yes. Image-heavy and illustrated books need extra care in both print and ebook; we handle placement, resolution and file size so everything looks right.

Do you format for Amazon KDP and IngramSpark?

Yes — and Apple Books, Kobo and more. We build to the exact requirements of each platform you publish on.

Do I keep my rights?

Always, 100%. Formatting your book with OW never transfers ownership. Your book, rights and royalties stay entirely yours.
