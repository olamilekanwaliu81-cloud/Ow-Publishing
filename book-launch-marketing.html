Why publishing isn’t the finish line

Here’s the truth no one tells first-time authors: getting your book live is the start, not the finish. Thousands of good books sell almost nothing because, after upload day, nothing happens. Nobody is working to put the book in front of readers.

Book marketing is the ongoing work that changes that — running ads, refining your keywords and categories, testing your description and price, and watching what actually moves copies across platforms.

Our Book Growth service does exactly this, month after month. And we keep it honest: your advertising budget is always yours, paid straight to the platforms, fully visible, and never marked up by us. You see what’s working in plain language.

Good to know

Launch & Marketing FAQs

My book is already published but not selling — can you help?

Yes. Book Growth works on any live book. We audit your listing, fix what’s holding it back, run ads and optimise — you don’t need to have published with us.

What’s the difference between the management fee and ad spend?

The management fee is our work — setting up, running and optimising your campaigns. Ad spend is your advertising budget, paid straight to platforms like Amazon. Ad spend is separate, fully transparent, and never marked up by us.

How much ad budget do I need?

You can start small and scale what works. We advise a sensible starting budget for your book and keep every naira or dollar visible to you.

Can you guarantee sales or a bestseller?

No honest marketer can guarantee sales — anyone who does isn’t being straight with you. What we guarantee is real, measured work and transparent reporting that gives your book its best shot.

Do I keep my rights and royalties?

Always, 100%. Marketing your book never affects ownership. Your rights and royalties stay entirely yours.
