What is book layout & typesetting?

Typesetting is the art of arranging your words on the page: the font and size, the spacing between lines, how chapters open, where page numbers sit, and how images and tables are placed. Layout design is the overall look and feel of your book’s interior.

A great interior is invisible — the reader never notices it, they just keep turning pages. A bad one is obvious: cramped margins, text falling into the spine, inconsistent headings, and that unmistakable “made in Word” feeling.

If you’re publishing your first book, this is where amateur and professional part ways. We typeset your interior for print and ebook, built to exact specifications, so it reads beautifully and passes platform review the first time.

Good to know

Layout & Typesetting FAQs

What’s the difference between layout, typesetting and formatting?

They overlap. Layout and typesetting are the craft of arranging your interior to look professional; formatting is preparing the final files to each platform’s spec. At OW they come together as one finished interior.

Can’t I just format my book in Word myself?

You can, but Word rarely produces a true print-ready interior — spacing, fonts and margins often get rejected at upload or look amateur in print. Professional typesetting is what makes a book feel real in the hand.

Do you handle images and tables?

Yes — photos, diagrams, tables, poetry and complex front/back matter are all placed and balanced so the interior reads cleanly in print and ebook.

Will it pass KDP and IngramSpark?

Yes. We build to exact trim, margin, gutter and bleed specs so your files are accepted the first time, with no upload rejections.

Do I keep my rights?

Always, 100%. Typesetting your book with OW never transfers ownership. Your book, rights and royalties stay entirely yours.
