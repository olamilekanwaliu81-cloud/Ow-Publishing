What does “book publishing” actually involve?

Publishing is far more than uploading a file. It’s choosing the right platforms, writing your metadata (the title info, categories and keywords that help readers find you), loading your print and ebook files correctly, setting pricing, and checking everything so your book goes live without errors.

For a first-time author, the dashboards alone are overwhelming — KDP, IngramSpark, Apple, Kobo each work differently. Small mistakes in categories or keywords can bury a good book where no one finds it.

We handle the whole setup for you, on the platforms that actually fit your book. And crucially: OW is a service you hire. You never sign your rights away — your accounts, your royalties and your book stay entirely yours.

Good to know

Book Publishing FAQs

Is OW my publisher? Do you take my rights?

No. OW is a service you hire, not an acquiring publisher. You keep 100% of your rights, your royalties and your publishing accounts. This is the opposite of a vanity press.

Which platforms should I publish on?

It depends on your book. Amazon KDP and IngramSpark are the usual core (ebook + wide print distribution), often with Apple Books, Kobo and others. We advise the right mix — you don’t need all of them.

What is metadata and why does it matter?

Metadata is your book’s title info, categories, keywords and description. It’s how readers and stores find your book. Good metadata is the difference between discoverable and invisible.

Can you publish a book that’s already written and designed?

Yes. If your files are ready we can go straight to publishing setup. If not, we can edit, format and design first, then publish.

Do I keep my royalties?

Yes — 100%. Royalties are paid by the platforms directly to your account. OW never sits between you and your earnings.
