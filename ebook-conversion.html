What is ebook conversion — and why not just a PDF?

Ebook conversion turns your manuscript into EPUB — the standard ebook format used by Amazon Kindle, Apple Books, Kobo and the rest. It’s what lets readers buy and read your book on a phone, tablet or e-reader.

A common first-timer mistake is uploading a PDF as an “ebook.” A PDF is fixed — it doesn’t reflow, so on a phone it becomes tiny and unreadable. A proper ebook adapts to any screen and any font size the reader chooses.

We convert your book to professional EPUB with a linked table of contents, proper chapter breaks and consistent styling, then test it so links work and images sit correctly across devices and stores.

Good to know

eBook Conversion FAQs

Can’t I just upload a PDF as my ebook?

You can, but you shouldn’t. A PDF doesn’t reflow, so on a phone it becomes tiny and frustrating to read. A proper EPUB adapts to any screen and font size — that’s what readers expect.

What is EPUB?

EPUB is the standard reflowable ebook format used by Apple Books, Kobo and most stores (Amazon converts it for Kindle). It lets your book adapt to any device and font size.

Will my images and formatting survive?

Yes. We rebuild your styling for ebook so headings, emphasis, block quotes and images all display cleanly — and we test it before it goes out.

Does it work on Amazon Kindle?

Yes. We supply a file Amazon KDP accepts and that displays correctly on Kindle devices and apps, as well as Apple Books, Kobo and others.

Do I keep my rights?

Always, 100%. Converting your ebook with OW never transfers ownership. Your book, rights and royalties stay entirely yours.
