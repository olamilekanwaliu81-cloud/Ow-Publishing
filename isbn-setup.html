ISBN setup & barcodes

Get the right ISBN set up the right way — so your book is registered correctly and the barcode sits perfectly on your cover.

Book a consultation → See pricing

An ISBN identifies your edition and tells stores who the publisher is. We help you decide whether to use a free platform ISBN or your own, explain what each means for your rights, and set it up correctly.

We also generate the correct barcode and place it on your cover to spec, so nothing gets rejected at upload.

What's included

ISBN setup includes

  • Guidance on free vs. your-own ISBN, and who shows as publisher.
  • ISBN assignment and registration help.
  • Correct EAN-13 barcode generated for print.
  • Barcode placed on your cover to platform spec.
  • Separate ISBNs handled for paperback, hardcover and ebook.
