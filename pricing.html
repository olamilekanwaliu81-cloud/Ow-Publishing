Before you commit

Pricing questions, answered

Why don't I see fixed prices everywhere?

Because no two books are the same. Page count, editing needs, illustration, format mix and platform choices all move the number. So we show clear "from" figures and give you an exact, itemised quote at your consultation, with nothing hidden.

Do I keep my rights, whatever I pay for?

Always, 100%. Every package, at every tier, leaves you owning your book, your rights and every copy. Paying OW for a service never transfers ownership; this is the opposite of a vanity press.

What's the difference between the management fee and "ad spend"?

The management fee is our work, setting up, running and optimising your campaigns. Ad spend is your advertising budget, paid straight to platforms like Amazon. Ad spend is separate, fully transparent, and never marked up by us.

Can I start with Production now and add Book Growth later?

Yes. Many authors publish first with a one-time Production package, then add the monthly Book Growth retainer once the book is live and they're ready to push sales. You can also start Growth on a book that's already published.

Can I buy just one piece instead of a whole package?

Yes. Cover design is offered on its own, and The Agent Route is available per deliverable, for example, just a query letter or a synopsis. You only pay for what you need.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Because we hold no stock, there's no warehousing markup buried in your cost. You see exactly what each service is for, and Local Print is quoted upfront before anything is printed.

What currency will I be billed in, and how do I pay?

We work with both foreign and local authors. We accept USD, Euro, Pounds, USDT, and the Naira equivalent, and we handle the currency-difference charge. Currency should never stop you from getting your book produced; we'll confirm the method that suits you.

How long until my book is ready?

It depends on scope, length, whether editing and illustration are involved, and how ready your manuscript is. We map a clear timeline with a finish line at your consultation, so you always know where things stand.
