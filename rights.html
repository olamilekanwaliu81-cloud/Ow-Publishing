Author Rights & Ownership Statement

Plain English · 2 min read

One short page, so there's no doubt: your book is yours. Working with OW Publishing House never changes that.

You keep 100% of your rights

Every right to your work stays with you, copyright, publishing rights, translation and adaptation rights, all of it. We do not take a share of your rights, and we never ask you to sign them over. When you publish through us, you remain the author and the owner, full stop.

You own every copy

We hold no stock. Whether it's a print-on-demand paperback on Amazon or a box of copies from a local print run, the books are yours. There is no OW warehouse sitting on your inventory, and no markup hidden in storage you never asked for.

We're a service, not an owner

OW is a house you hire, for production, cover design, publishing setup, growth, agent preparation or local print. Paying us for a service never transfers ownership of your book to us. This is the opposite of a vanity press, which dresses a paid service up as "being selected" and quietly keeps your rights.

You keep your royalties

Your books are published under your own accounts wherever possible, so your royalties are paid to you, by the platforms, directly. On the Book Growth retainer you pay us a transparent management fee; your advertising spend and your royalties remain entirely yours.

You stay in control

You approve your cover, your interior, your metadata and your pricing. Nothing goes live without you. And if you ever want to take your book elsewhere, you can, because it was always yours to move.

Produce it. Publish it. Grow it. It stays entirely your own.

If anything here is ever unclear, ask us, plainly, and we'll answer plainly. Get in touch or book a consultation.
