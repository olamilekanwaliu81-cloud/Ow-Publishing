Good questions

Self-publishing FAQ

Do I keep my rights?

Yes — 100%. You always own your book, your rights and every copy. OW never takes ownership or warehouses stock; we produce, publish and grow, and it stays yours throughout.

Which platforms will my book be on?

It depends on the book. We advise the right mix from the top platforms — Amazon KDP and IngramSpark are the usual backbone, with Apple Books, Kobo, Barnes & Noble, Google Play and others added where they fit. An ebook-first novel, a children's picture book and a bookstore-targeted memoir each get a different mix.

How long does production take?

Timelines depend on scope — length, whether editorial and illustration are involved, and how ready the manuscript is. We map a clear timeline with a finish line at your consultation, so you always know where the project stands.

What does "ad spend" mean?

Book Growth has two parts: our management fee (setting up and running the campaigns) and your ad spend (the budget paid to platforms like Amazon to show your ads). Ad spend is separate, transparent and yours — paid to the platform directly, never marked up.

Can I start with just Production and add Growth later?

Absolutely. Many authors begin with a one-time Production project to get published properly, then add the Book Growth retainer once the book is live and they're ready to push sales. You can start Growth on an already-published book too.
