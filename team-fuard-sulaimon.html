Fuard Sulaimon runs the work most publishers skip: turning a live listing into real, tracked sales. He leads OW’s Book Growth retainer, from Amazon Ads setup and keyword strategy to cross-platform sales monitoring and launch campaigns.

He treats every campaign as measurable: small, deliberate changes to ads, keywords, descriptions and pricing, watched closely and reported back in plain language, so authors can see what’s actually moving copies.

Fuard’s focus is simple, honest growth, with the author’s ad budget always paid to the platforms directly and never marked up.
