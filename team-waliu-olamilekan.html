Waliu Olamilekan

Founder & CEO

Leadership

Waliu Olamilekan founded OW Publishing House on a simple idea: an author should be able to produce a professional book, publish it on the right platforms, and actually grow its sales, without ever signing away their rights or their copies.

With 3+ years in book production and a certified KDP and IngramSpark workflow behind real published titles, Waliu leads the house’s strategy and stays personally involved in the books OW takes on, from the first consultation to the finished cover.

He built OW to be lean and honest by design: no warehouse, no inventory, no rights grab, just a joined-up team and a founder you can actually talk to.
