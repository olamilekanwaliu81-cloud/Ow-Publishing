1. What we do

2. Quotes & payment

Prices are custom to each book and confirmed in your quote. [Payment schedule, deposit and accepted methods to be confirmed by OW.] For the Book Growth retainer, our management fee is separate from your advertising spend, which you pay to the platforms directly.

You keep 100% of your rights and every copy of your book. Paying us for a service never transfers ownership to us. See our Author Rights & Ownership Statement for the full promise.

You confirm that the work you send us is yours to publish and doesn't infringe anyone else's rights. You're responsible for approving your final files — cover, interior, metadata and pricing — before anything goes live.
